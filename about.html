About — Amber Morrey

The architect of the considered wardrobe.

Amber Morrey began as Melbourne's “frugal fashionista” — proof that style was never about spending more, but choosing better. Over a decade of styling, that instinct sharpened into a philosophy.

Today she works with a small number of clients to build wardrobes the way an architect designs a home: on a foundation, in materials that age with grace, around a silhouette that is unmistakably theirs.

Her practice is rooted in sustainability — not as a slogan, but as the natural outcome of buying less and buying well. The result is a wardrobe that serves you for a decade, and a presence that arrives before you speak.
